Our Story

For many Filipinos, having a better life means working harder, sacrificing time with family, or looking for opportunities beyond the usual 9-to-5 job.

Some are OFWs working far from home.

Some are parents who want to contribute financially while staying with their family.

Some are students looking for ways to earn and gain valuable skills.

Others are employees and entrepreneurs looking for another source of income.

But one question is often the same.

“Is there another way?”

This is where Online Business Academy (OBA) comes in.

For more than 12 years, OBA has been creating an online community where Filipinos can learn about online marketing, develop digital skills, and explore the possibilities of building an online business.

OBA was built around a simple idea: you don't have to be an expert to start. You just have to be willing to learn.

Our community is made up of ordinary people — people with families to support, dreams to pursue, and goals they want to achieve. Some started with little knowledge about online business. Some were simply curious. Others were looking for a way to make better use of their time and skills.

What they had in common was the courage to take the first step.

From Learning to Building

It isn't magic, and it isn't overnight.

Building an online business takes learning, action, patience, and consistency.

That's why OBA focuses not only on the opportunity, but also on helping people understand the process and become part of a community where they can learn and grow.

Because sometimes, the biggest change doesn't happen when everything is already figured out. It happens when you finally decide:

“I'll give myself a chance.”
